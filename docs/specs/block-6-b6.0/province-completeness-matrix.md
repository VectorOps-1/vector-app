## Composition Rule

For a company operating in more than one province:

1. Evaluate the national baseline once against the selected national pack version.
2. Evaluate each provincial overlay separately against its own version and jurisdiction.
3. Do not merge conflicting provincial requirements.
4. Show the source and jurisdiction beside every finding.
5. If any selected province is incomplete, show `Source pack incomplete` for that province and withhold an inspection-ready claim for it.

## Province Gate Decision

No province is approved or active. Western Cape is the only province with
enough retained legal text to prepare a candidate overlay for legal and
operational review. Gauteng, Free State, KwaZulu-Natal and Eastern Cape have
useful process evidence, but process evidence is not an inspection checklist.
North West now also has current process-listing evidence, although the
underlying notice and complete pack were not publicly obtainable. No province
is ready for authoritative client evaluation.
