## Closure Rules

An entry closes only when the exact supplied record is retained with sender and
authority metadata, hash, version, effective date, replacement status, usage
conditions and independent extraction evidence. A refusal, non-existence
confirmation or confidentiality decision must also be retained and escalated
through its stated PAIA/review route.

No entry closes from a search snippet, portal label, old operator memory,
tender condition, another province's document or AI inference.
