# B6.0 Qualified Legal Review Dossier

Status: **PREPARED - REVIEW NOT PERFORMED**

## Requested Reviewer

A South African attorney or advocate with current health-regulatory and administrative-law competence. Provincial licensing experience is required for each provincial overlay decision.

## Review Questions

1. Confirm current legal force, commencement, amendment, repeal, and supersession of every candidate source.
2. Resolve the relationship among the 2017 national EMS Regulations, 2022 EMS Standards, provincial instruments, licence conditions, and any OHSC measurement tool.
3. Determine whether and how the Western Cape 2010 Act and 2012 Regulations remain operative.
4. Confirm applicability to private ground ambulance, response, rescue, aeromedical, volunteer, event, and planned transport services.
5. Confirm provincial authority, process, appeals, inspections, enforcement, and annual renewal obligations.
6. Classify forms, annexures, fees, directives, policies, portals, and departmental practice by legal effect.
7. Confirm medicine licence/permit, procurement, possession, storage, administration, registers, destruction, and practitioner-scope boundaries.
8. Confirm staff registration, annual practising evidence, CPD, scope, clinical guidelines, and manager requirements.
9. Confirm vehicle registration, roadworthiness, markings, lights/sirens, PrDP, safety, maintenance, and licence-token duties.
10. Confirm base/building, fire, utilities, security, waste, exposure, IPC, records, complaints, incidents, and data-submission duties.
11. Confirm patient, staff, operational, licence, medicine, and evidence retention periods from exact sources.
12. Identify each item as law, regulation, licence condition, mandatory standard, official form, inspection practice, guidance, or internal control.
13. Approve or reject each candidate requirement and its plain-English wording.

## Materials Supplied

- acquisition and missing-source remediation reports;
- retained-file inventory, hashes and exact archived issuing-URL national PDFs;
- page-anchored 2022 OCR and NDoH assessment/framework status extracts;
- national, Western Cape and provincial-process candidate extractions;
- independent verification record;
- province completeness matrix;
- unresolved-source list and source request pack;
- proposed applicability/exclusion matrix and client-facing limitations.

Portal pages, adverts, newsletters and historical notices are supplied only to
prove process/contact facts. They may not become inspection criteria unless the
applicable authority and legal effect are established.

## Required Deliverable

Return a signed decision table containing source ID/hash, clause/page, jurisdiction, service applicability, legal classification, decision, effective dates, limitations, conflicts, reviewer credentials, and date. A general approval statement is insufficient.

The reviewer must separately decide whether archived issuing-URL copies are
authentic/current enough for clause review, whether a newer consolidated text
or assessment instrument exists, and whether commercial quotation/display is
permitted. Unanswered authority requests remain visible gaps, not inferred
approvals.

## Cost Control

- Obtain a written fixed-fee or capped quote before review.
- Quote the national baseline separately from each provincial overlay.
- Quote Western Cape first; quote other provinces only after their source packs are complete.
- Do not pay for requirement drafting from missing or unverified sources.
- Retain the engagement letter, scope, exclusions, fee, and professional-indemnity details.
