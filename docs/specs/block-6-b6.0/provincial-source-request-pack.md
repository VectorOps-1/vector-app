# B6.0 National And Provincial Source Request Pack

Status: **DRAFT REQUEST MATERIAL - NOT SENT**

These requests seek current records used for private-EMS licensing and annual
inspection. They do not assert that a missing record exists, that a portal is
law, or that AcuityOps has legal approval.

## Standard Request

Subject: Request for current private EMS licensing and annual inspection source pack

Request the current official, version-dated private emergency medical service material for the province:

1. new-service application form and instructions;
2. annual renewal form and submission timetable;
3. service and vehicle licence categories;
4. current fee schedule;
5. annual inspection checklist or measurement tool;
6. documentary evidence checklist;
7. ambulance, response, rescue, base, communications, personnel, equipment, medicine, stock, waste, and record criteria used during inspection;
8. current directives, circulars, policies, and district conditions;
9. amendment, replacement, or withdrawal notices affecting older provincial instruments;
10. responsible Licensing and Inspectorate contact details;
11. confirmation of how the 2017 national EMS Regulations and 2022 EMS Standards are applied in current provincial licensing practice;
12. confirmation whether the supplied documents may be retained and cited in a compliance-readiness software pack.

Ask the Department to identify confidential or non-public material explicitly. Do not publish confidential inspection material without permission.

Please identify each supplied document's title, version, approval authority,

record currently used instead. If a record is withheld, identify the legal
basis, decision-maker and review/appeal process. Identify all usage,
confidentiality and redistribution conditions.

## Receipt Procedure

For every response:

1. retain the original email, headers and attachments;
2. record sender identity, role, department, and official domain;
3. hash every attachment;
4. record usage, confidentiality and redistribution restrictions;
5. do not overwrite older versions;
6. classify source type, jurisdiction, applicability and effective dates;
7. link every extracted clause to the exact retained source and page, then
   route it through independent checking, legal review and province-specific
   operational review;
8. never activate a requirement directly from an email statement without a stable authority and applicability record.
