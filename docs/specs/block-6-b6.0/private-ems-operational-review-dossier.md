# B6.0 Private-EMS Operational Review Dossier

Status: **PREPARED - REVIEW NOT PERFORMED**

## Requested Review Panel

- private EMS licence holder or accountable executive;
- experienced private EMS operations manager;
- currently registered Emergency Care practitioner with governance responsibility;
- fleet, equipment, and medicine custodian where the panel lacks that competence;
- reviewer with direct inspection experience in the applicable province.

Each reviewer must declare employer, province, service type, registration, conflicts, and whether an observation is written authority or local practice.

The first operational review must include a reviewer with recent inspection or
licensing experience in the exact province proposed for activation. A review
in one province does not validate another province's overlay.

## Operational Walkthrough

Review every candidate requirement across:

1. company licence and provincial operating authority;
2. bases, substations, satellite points, communications, utilities, security, and fire readiness;
3. ambulances, response/rescue vehicles, markings, roadworthiness, tokens, maintenance, cleaning, and damage records;
4. equipment availability, function, service/calibration, manufacturer schedules, and allocation;
5. medicine authorisation, schedule control, stock, expiry, storage, temperature, registers, losses, and destruction;
6. consumable stock, minimum holdings, issue/allocation, shortages, and evidence;
7. staff identity, qualification, HPCSA status, CPD, scope, driver licence/PrDP, roster, exposure, training, and files;
8. patient records, response data, complaints, adverse incidents, governance, handover, and monthly submissions;
9. IPC, decontamination, linen, sharps, risk waste, occupational exposure, and contractor evidence;
10. disaster, event, and interfacility-transfer applicability;
11. what inspectors physically check and what evidence they accept;
12. realistic action priority, owner, due date, and effort for a failed item.

## Required Decision Per Item

- correct as written;
- legally sourced but operational wording unclear;
- not applicable to the service type;
- evidence type unrealistic or incomplete;
- frequency incorrect or unverified;
- local practice only, not a documented requirement;
- source conflict;
- missing requirement;
- reject pending legal clarification.

## Plain-English Output Test

For every approved item, confirm that a manager can understand what is required, why it matters, its source and province, missing evidence, the AcuityOps record expected, the first action, the action owner, and whether the item is mandatory, conditional, guidance, or unresolved.

No remembered inspector preference may become an authoritative rule without a retained source or formally recorded licence condition.

Portal fields, advert categories and tender specifications may test workflow
coverage, but may not be presented as annual inspection criteria without the
retained governing source and legal decision. The panel must identify every
difference between written authority, forms, actual inspection practice and
evidence normally accepted by inspectors.
