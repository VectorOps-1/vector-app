Core licensing, management, annual inspection, display, information, and annexure topics are present in both source sets. Formatting and consolidation differences prevent a byte-for-byte comparison.

This is an independent document-source comparison, not independent legal approval. A human checker must compare every candidate clause and annexure field against the retained official pages before `SourceVerified` is assigned.

## Current Process Evidence

The current portal and retained adverts show that applications can distinguish
new services, relocations, extensions, bases, ambulances, response vehicles,
rescue/other vehicles and service-level changes, and are published for public
comment. This supports a future application workflow only. It does not disclose
the current annual inspection checklist, evidence criteria, fee schedule or
legal interaction with the national instruments, and creates no inspection
rule.

## Unresolved Legal Question

The 2017 national Regulations expressly repealed the 2015 national EMS Regulations, but the retained national text does not expressly decide the continued operation of the 2012 Western Cape instrument. Qualified legal review and written provincial confirmation are required. AcuityOps must not silently apply both instruments or choose one over the other.
