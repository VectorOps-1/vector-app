## 2022 Candidate Domains

The 2022 instrument is separately classified as national service standards. It applies to public and private EMS and commenced 12 months after promulgation. Candidate domains include:

- user rights, information, satisfaction, and complaints;
- healthcare-user records and clinical governance;
- call management, dispatch, response, interfacility transfer, and patient safety;
- infection prevention, waste, medicines/supplies, and medical-equipment management;
- oversight, governance, human resources, occupational health and safety, and disaster readiness;
- fleet, buildings, utilities, security, and linen.

These domains are not converted into annual-licensing requirements in B6.0. Legal review must determine their relationship to provincial licensing and any applicable measurement tool.

## National Assessment-Tool Status Evidence

The retained 2024/25 NDoH Annual Performance Plan records a national output
concerning assessment of all nine provinces against the Regulations Relating
to Standards for EMS and references assessment-tool development/revision. The
retained 2026/27 plan confirms the 2017 Regulations and 2022 Standards as the
current framework described by the Department and records that EMS Regulations
are under revision. These are planning/status statements, not the assessment
instrument itself and not proof that a particular provincial licensing
inspector uses a specific checklist.

Candidate consequence: AcuityOps may record that a national assessment process
exists, but it may not reproduce or claim conformance to the missing tool until
the Department supplies the final version, authority, effective date and usage
scope.
